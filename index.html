Why now

The economics of trust have moved

For a decade, trust institutions competed on investment performance and fee levels. Fee compression has made that a race with no winner. The differentiator has shifted to the quality, consistency, and defensibility of trust administration itself.

Fee pressureWhen pricing converges, institutions differentiate on fiduciary quality of administration — controls, consistency, explainability.
Document-firstEvery investment posture and fee must reconcilable to the governing instrument, regulation, and case law.
Account watchTrust accounts concentrate the population and asset profile targeted by elder financial exploitation patterns.
Platform map

Governance is not the wrapper.
It is the architecture.

Agents at the core — with fiduciary duty, ethics, compliance, cybersecurity, and advisory regulation built into how they reason, not bolted on afterward.

Core

Reasoning Engine

Shared context: instruments, firm policies, regulation, prior decisions. Agents do not carry a private view of the rules.

Compose

Agent Studio

Governed agents from an Action Catalog. Each action declares what it may read, propose, and where a human must stop it.

Systems

The Bridge

One auditable doorway to systems of record. Read first. Write only after human approval.

Documents

Trust Intelligence

Document intake, cited abstracts, human review gates. Abstracts cite. They do not conclude.

Evidence

The Record

Per-account hash chain. A public fingerprint only — never a client file.

Interface

Natural language

Conversational layer over existing systems. No rip-and-replace. Questions stay inside the same gates.
