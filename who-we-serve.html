Trustees and custodians below.

r.Team sits between fiduciary practices and the trustees and custodians they depend on. The platform does not replace the trustee of record or the custodian of assets. It is the reasoning layer between them.

Practices that run on the platform

01

Professional trustees

Independent and directed appointments that need consistent, defensible administration.

02

Trust companies

Chartered fiduciary institutions competing on quality of administration under fee pressure.

03

Law firms

Trusts, estates, and counsel who need cited governing terms and a clean decision record.

04

RIAs

Advisors with fiduciary clients, working the same layer with the trust team.

05

Insurance carriers

Fiduciary and settlement workflows that must stay reconcilable to governing documents.

06

Structured settlements

Specialized fiduciary practices that still need proposal, citation, and human approval.

Also built for bank wealth & trust

Bank wealth and trust departments face the same shift: fee compression, documentation scrutiny, and account-level risk. r.Team peers administrators with private reasoning under their own governance.

  • Interpretation governance — every reading of a provision becomes an auditable record
  • Parallel-run independence — prove consistency beside legacy systems before switching
  • Per-institution isolation — dedicated storage, database, and compute where required

Engagement posture

Work proceeds as a consulting and service engagement under the institution’s governance, one step at a time — not a black-box swap of fiduciary judgment.
